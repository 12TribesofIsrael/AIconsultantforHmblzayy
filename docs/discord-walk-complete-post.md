## Alternate verse

If Mark 9:23 has already been used in the channel, this is the other "all things are
possible" text, also verbatim from the 1611:

> *"But Jesus beheld [them,] and said unto them, With men this is impossible; but with God all things are possible."*
> — Matthew 19:26, KJV 1611

Mark 9:23 is the better fit here: it puts the possibility on the believer's side of the
line, which is what a man who walked 3,000 miles on a promise actually demonstrated.
Matthew 19:26 is about God's power over human limits — true, but it's the answer to a
different question.
